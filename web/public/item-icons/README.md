## Selection conventions

Every armor, weapon, item and consumable entry has an explicit `image` assignment.
Only referenced SVGs are included, including older artwork retained for saved
equipment and historical chat. Weapon and armor upgrades retain the base
family's silhouette. Harrowbone uses ribbed body armor, longswords use a straight
two-handed blade, and quarterstaffs use a fighting staff. Equipment distinguishes
blades, axes, polearms, bows, crossbows, firearms, shields, gauntlets, robes,
leather, mail, scales and plate.
Recipes use scrolls, not drinkable-potion artwork. Ordinary potions share a
bottle family; healing, stamina/magical tonics and poisons have distinct artwork.
Other consumables retain recognizable forms such as tea, salve jars, powders,
seeds, roots, flowers, mushrooms, food and shards.

Custom inventory items reuse the chest, backpack, standing-potion and gem-pendant
icons for loot, gear, consumables and class items, respectively.

The supplied collection has no literal wheelchair, concealed cane-sword or lute
icon. Their nearest available silhouettes are the wheeled `office-chair`,
`telescopic-baton` and `guitar`, respectively. Relics whose physical appearance
is unspecified use a consistent runestone motif. Named magical variants otherwise
keep their recognizable physical object instead of implying new mechanics.

All selected assets use `viewBox="0 0 512 512"` and white path geometry, without
transforms, strokes or other visible SVG element types.
